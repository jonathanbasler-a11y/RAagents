DIGITAL HUMAN HYBRID TEAM - DEMO (Mac)
25 AI agents help with a regulatory due diligence. All data is fictional.
It runs on your own computer. The people who built it do not see what you do.

THE 3 STEPS
  1. Unzip the ZIP and open the folder "HybridDemo".
  2. Double-click "Start practice demo". Your browser opens the demo.
  3. When you are done, double-click "Stop demo".

FIRST TIME ONLY
  The Mac may say the file "cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it".
  Click Done (not Move to Trash). Open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
  scroll down to the message about "Start practice demo", click Open Anyway
  and confirm. No administrator rights needed. It may ask again for another
  file, or for access to your folder: do the same, or click OK.

PRACTICE OR LIVE
  Practice: no key, no network. Every reply is a stand-in, not AI. It says so.
  Live: real AI answers. Every question you type, and the documents of the
  data room, go to the model gateway you set up. Use the fictional case or
  public documents only. Never type anything confidential.

FOR LIVE: FILL IN my-settings.env
  Double-click "Start live demo". The file opens. Fill in 3 lines:
    LLM_BASE_URL  the address of your gateway, up to and including /v1
    LLM_API_KEY   your own key only (ask your IT or AI team where to get one)
    LLM_MODEL     the exact model name
  Save, then double-click "Start live demo" again. Never share this file.

YOUR DATA is in the folder "data". To start again from empty: stop the demo,
then delete the "data" folder. TO REMOVE EVERYTHING: stop the demo, then
delete the folder "HybridDemo".

IF A SECURITY SETTING BLOCKS THE DOUBLE-CLICK
  Open Terminal, type cd and a space, drag the folder "HybridDemo" into the
  window, press Return. Then type this and press Return:
    ./runtime/darwin-arm64/bin/node launcher/bundle/launcher.mjs practice
  Intel Mac: use darwin-x64 instead. For live or stop, change the last word.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Browser did not open          Open the address shown in the window.
  "Not reachable" (live)        Connect to your company network or VPN.
  "Wrong key", "unknown model"  Check my-settings.env.
