# DR-08 Safety summary

Fictional practice document. Invented for an exercise; it describes no real company, asset or person.

Prepared by the Licensor. Dated 2026-07-25. STUDY-301 safety set: 118 participants (79 PRC-204, 39 placebo).

## Overview

- Most adverse events were mild or moderate. Headache and nausea were the most common.
- Two serious adverse events were reported, one in each arm. Neither was fatal.

## Liver enzymes

- ALT above three times the upper limit of normal: 4 of 79 participants on PRC-204 (5.1%), 0 of 39 on placebo.
- All four resolved. Two interrupted treatment and restarted; two continued without interruption.
- The Licensor assessed all four events as "unrelated to treatment". No independent adjudication has been reported.

## Exposure

Median exposure 24 weeks. Long-term safety data will come from STUDY-302.
