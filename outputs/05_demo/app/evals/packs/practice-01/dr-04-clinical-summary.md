## Design

Randomised 2:1, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 24 weeks. 118 adults randomised (79 PRC-204, 39 placebo).

## Topline table

| Endpoint | PRC-204 | Placebo | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Change in functional score at week 24 (primary) | +1.6 | -0.5 | difference 2.1 points, 95% CI 0.3 to 3.9, p = 0.02 |
| Time to loss of ambulation | not assessed | not assessed | the study is too short for this endpoint |
| Quality-of-life questionnaire | not collected | not collected | not part of the protocol |

## Notes

- The primary endpoint was met. There was one primary endpoint and it was pre-specified.
- Two participants in each arm had a protocol deviation that did not change their analysis population.

## Open-label extension

STUDY-302 enrols completers of STUDY-301. The first interim analysis is planned for the fourth quarter of 2026. No extension results have been reported.
