# DR-02 Agency pre-submission meeting minutes (excerpt)

Fictional practice document. Invented for an exercise; it describes no real company, asset or person.

Minutes of a pre-submission meeting between the Licensor and the agency, held 2026-03-18. Minutes issued 2026-03-20. Excerpt, three questions.

## Preliminaries

The agency confirmed at the start of the meeting that PRC-204 holds breakthrough therapy designation, granted 2025-04-22, for the adult indication.

## Question 1: Is one pivotal study enough?

Licensor position: STUDY-301 will be the single pivotal study for approval.

Agency response: A single study may be sufficient if it is supported by confirmatory evidence of effectiveness, for example a well-matched natural-history comparison or strong mechanistic evidence. The agency did not agree that STUDY-301 alone would suffice. The agency noted that a second study could be requested during review.

## Question 2: Paediatric requirements

Agency response: The Licensor should submit an initial paediatric study plan before the application is filed. The agency has not yet received or agreed a plan.

## Question 3: Dose and population

Agency response: The proposed dose and the adult population are acceptable for the pivotal study.

## Follow-up actions

- Licensor to submit an initial paediatric study plan.
- Licensor to propose the confirmatory evidence for the single-study approach.
