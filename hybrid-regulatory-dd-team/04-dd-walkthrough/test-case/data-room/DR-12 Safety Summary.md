## Hepatic safety statement (company)
"No cases meeting Hy's law criteria have been observed."

## IND safety report (2026-04-22)
The Phase 1/2 data listing shows one patient (cohort B) with ALT above 3x ULN, total bilirubin above 2x ULN, no cholestasis, and no alternative cause identified at the time of reporting; the event resolved after treatment interruption. The report classifies it as a "potential Hy's law case."

## Risk management
FDA stated it will evaluate the need for a REMS at review (DR-06 Excerpt A, Q5). No REMS proposal has been drafted.
