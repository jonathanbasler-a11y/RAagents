# DR-04 Clinical Development Summary: OST-4417

## Studies

| Study | Phase | Design | N | Status (2026-10) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OST-4417-101 | 1/2 | Dose escalation, then biomarker-selected expansion cohorts (A, B) | 142 total; cohort B n=68 | Cohort B data cutoff 2026-03-31; CSR synopsis dated 2026-06-18 |
| OST-4417-301 | 3 | Randomized, open-label, 1:1 OST-4417 150 mg QD vs investigator's choice chemotherapy; TRX-7-high; previously treated | 420 planned; 388 enrolled as of 2026-09-15 | Enrollment completes about Dec 2026 |

## OST-4417-301 pivotal design

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Primary endpoint | Progression-free survival (PFS) by blinded independent central review (BICR), per protocol v4.0 (2025-02-24) |
| Key secondary | Overall survival (OS), ORR, DoR, safety, patient-reported outcomes |
| Prior protocol | v3.0 (2024-06-10): OS primary, PFS key secondary |
| Statistical plan | Event-driven; interim PFS analysis at about 60% of events, projected Q2 2027; final PFS and OS later |
| Crossover | Not permitted in v4.0 |
| Biomarker testing | Clinical trial assay (CTA) version 2 at central lab |
| Regions | US, EU (6 countries), South Korea; no Japan sites |

## Phase 2 cohort B efficacy: data versions

The company's two data versions differ (see DR-15 for the table).

| Version | Cutoff | Evaluable | ORR | Median DoR | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Investor deck (DR-02) | 2025-09-30 | 61 | 46% (28/61) | 9.8 mo | Investigator-assessed, includes unconfirmed |
| CSR synopsis (this document) | 2026-03-31 | 68 | 40% (27/68) | 8.4 mo (95% CI 5.6 to 11.2) | BICR-assessed, confirmed responses only |

Median PFS in cohort B: 5.9 months (95% CI 4.7 to 7.3). Median OS not mature (data cutoff 2026-03-31, 41% of patients with an event).

## Investigator's Brochure

IB version 6.0 (2026-04) is current. Protocol v4.0 and the 2026 DSURs cite IB v5.0 for the reference safety information. Version alignment has not been documented.

## Interim and exploratory notes
- A pre-specified DILI (drug-induced liver injury) monitoring plan applies in both studies.
- One patient in cohort B had ALT above 3x ULN with total bilirubin above 2x ULN and no alternative cause identified; reported as a potential Hy's law case in the 2026-04 IND safety report (see DR-12).
