# DR-06 Agency Meeting Minutes (excerpts)

## Excerpt A: FDA Type B end-of-Phase 2 meeting, 2024-03-14 (minutes issued 2024-04-12)

**Question 1 (pivotal design).** Sponsor proposed a randomized trial of OST-4417 vs investigator's choice chemotherapy in TRX-7-high disease.
- FDA response: "FDA recommends overall survival as the primary endpoint. Progression-free survival may be a key secondary endpoint. If the Sponsor intends to propose PFS as the primary endpoint, the Sponsor should submit justification, including data on the expected relationship to OS, and FDA will review the proposal; this should not be interpreted as agreement."

**Question 3 (accelerated approval).** Sponsor asked whether ORR and DoR from cohort B of study 101 could support accelerated approval.
- FDA response: "The Agency cannot agree in advance that ORR and DoR will support accelerated approval. The review will depend on the magnitude and durability of response in the biomarker-selected population and on available therapy at the time of submission. A randomized confirmatory trial should be underway at the time of submission."

**Question 4 (companion diagnostic).**
- FDA response: "A contemporaneously approved in vitro companion diagnostic is expected. The Sponsor should engage CDRH through a pre-submission. The assay used to select patients in the trial should be the final assay or bridged to it with adequate clinical evidence."

**Question 5 (hepatic safety).**
- FDA response: "Provide a DILI monitoring and stopping-rule plan. The Agency will evaluate the need for a REMS or other risk management at the time of review."

**Question 6 (CMC).**
- FDA response: "Provide a comparability assessment for the change in the drug substance synthetic route (route A to route B) before the pivotal study uses route B material."

## Excerpt B: EMA Scientific Advice, 2024-09-19 (final advice 2024-10-04)

- "The CHMP advises that OS is the preferred primary endpoint. A PFS primary endpoint requires justification and a plan for OS maturity."
- "A conditional marketing authorization may be considered if the benefit-risk is positive and comprehensive data can be provided later; this is not a commitment."
- "The Applicant is reminded that a Paediatric Investigation Plan agreement, or a waiver, is required for validation of a marketing authorization application. No PIP application has been submitted as of the date of this meeting."

## Excerpt C: FDA response to Breakthrough Therapy designation request, 2025-11-03

- "The Agency has determined that the preliminary clinical evidence does not indicate that the drug may demonstrate substantial improvement over available therapy on a clinically significant endpoint. The Sponsor may resubmit with additional, more mature data."
